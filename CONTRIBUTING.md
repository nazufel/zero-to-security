# Contributing

This course stays useful because people tell us what's wrong with it.

## The easiest ways to help

- **Something confused you?** [Open a "This was confusing" issue.](../../issues/new?template=confusing.md) If you were confused, others were too.
- **A link broke or a fact is out of date?** [Open a "Broken link or outdated fact" issue.](../../issues/new?template=outdated.md)
- **Are you a hiring manager or working in security?** [Share interview questions.](../../issues/new?template=interview-question.md) Real questions from real interviews are the most valuable thing you can give this course.

## Writing or editing lessons

1. Read [STYLE.md](STYLE.md). Every lesson follows its template.
2. Keep it lean. Every lesson must serve a box on its module's "ready to move on" checklist.
3. Only free resources (the [free AI path](appendix/free-ai-path.md) must keep working).
4. Add a `Last verified` date to anything that can change.
5. Test every command yourself on a fresh Ubuntu VM before you open a pull request.

## What we'll say no to

- Topics that don't help someone get an entry-level job. They might fit in [Dig Deeper](dig-deeper/).
- Paid tools, courses, or certifications as requirements.
- Walls of text.
