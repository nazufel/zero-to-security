# Module 10 quiz

This module's quiz is about **saying** things, so answer every question out loud, timed, before you open the answer.

1. A hiring manager gives your portfolio one minute. What must they be able to find?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   What you do, the job you want, and links to proof, organized so they can click straight to your strongest work for that role.
   </details>

2. Turn "Familiar with AWS IAM" into a strong resume bullet.
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Something like: "Designed least-privilege IAM for a serverless app: per-function roles, group-based team access, and a view-only AI agent role, verified with a policy-simulator test suite." Action, what, how, proof.
   </details>

3. Give your 60-second pitch. (Time it.)
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Check it has: where you're from (10s), what you did (20s), one specific proof with a result (20s), and why this role (10s).
   </details>

4. "Tell me about a time you made a mistake." Go.
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   A STAR story about a real mistake from this course, like locking yourself out of SSH, with what you learned and what you do differently now. Honest and specific beats polished.
   </details>

5. "Aren't you worried AI will replace you?" Go.
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   It covers: AI makes you faster (specifically how), you check its work (a real example), you keep it in a box (your controls), and why security needs people who can do all three.
   </details>

6. An interviewer asks about a tool you've never used. What do you say?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   What you know that's related, how you'd learn the rest, and where you'd look. Never bluff.
   </details>

7. Should you get a certification before applying?
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   It depends on your market: count how often your target postings ask for one. If most do, or you're targeting government work, it's probably worth it. If not, apply with your portfolio.
   </details>

8. Rewrite "Kubernetes expert" honestly.
   <details><summary>Answer</summary>

   Something like: "Hands-on lab experience with Kubernetes (kind): deployed my own containerized tool, wrote RBAC and network policies, reviewed AI-written manifests for privilege escalation."
   </details>

## Challenge

Do the [Claude mock interview](../appendix/mock-interviews.md#practice-with-claude) for your target role, all ten questions, out loud, recorded. Then listen back and write down the one answer you'd most like to redo. Redo it tomorrow.
